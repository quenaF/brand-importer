# Human Outcome

## Practical task

Help a developer or product team import an authorized brand into a white-label product without manually reconstructing its identity from scattered website evidence.

## Human outcome

The developer becomes able to represent another organization with confidence, speed, and integrity because the importer makes clear what is known, what is inferred, what remains unresolved, and what the brand owner has confirmed or corrected.

## Product role

Brand Importer is not the hero. It helps the developer create an experience in which the brand owner recognizes themselves and the end user experiences a coherent, trustworthy product.

## Experience hypothesis

If the importer separates direct evidence from interpretation, preserves provenance, and gives the owner meaningful correction authority, developers can move faster without treating approximation as truth.

## Non-goals

Brand Importer does not:

- certify ownership of a brand;
- reproduce a brand without authorization;
- infer emotional intent as fact;
- replace brand strategy, legal review, accessibility review, or owner approval;
- claim that visual similarity alone equals brand fidelity.

## Evidence status

This document is **Observed—plan**. It describes the intended human outcome for the product, not verified user behavior.
