# Moments That Matter

## 1. Starting an import

The user supplies a URL or source and states their authorization relationship. The system must explain what it will inspect and what it will not establish.

## 2. Discovering available evidence

The importer inventories pages, stylesheets, assets, fonts, metadata, and inaccessible sources before interpreting the brand. Missing access remains visible.

## 3. Seeing the first extraction

The user sees direct observations separately from normalized candidates. The interface must not make early guesses look final.

## 4. Encountering ambiguity

Conflicting colors, fonts, logos, messages, or outdated sources are surfaced with context. The importer should show why a decision is uncertain and what would resolve it.

## 5. Reviewing experience interpretation

Voice, promise, emotional outcomes, trust principles, and recovery behavior are presented as hypotheses unless directly supplied or owner confirmed.

## 6. Correcting the importer

The owner can confirm, correct, replace, deprioritize, or mark unknown. Corrections must be understandable, reversible, and preserved with provenance.

## 7. Generating the Brand Pack

The user knows whether the pack is provisional, owner reviewed, or blocked by material unknowns. Generation must not erase conflicts or unresolved decisions.

## 8. Applying the brand

Exporters consume the canonical Brand Pack rather than reinterpreting the source. Preview differences should be attributable to tokens and rules.

## 9. Handling failure

Blocked pages, malformed CSS, missing assets, invalid schemas, exporter errors, and inaccessible color pairings must produce a clear next step without destroying collected work.

## 10. Re-importing after change

The importer compares new evidence with the existing pack, preserves owner corrections, and asks before replacing consequential confirmed decisions.

## Evidence status

These moments are **Observed—plan**. Dogfooding must verify which moments create the greatest confusion, delay, or trust risk.
