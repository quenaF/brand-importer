# Design Principles

## Evidence before elegance

A beautiful output is not successful if the importer cannot explain where it came from.

## Transparency over confidence

Show uncertainty, conflict, missing access, and unresolved decisions at the point they matter. Confidence scores may supplement evidence status but never replace it.

## Owner authority without evidence erasure

Owner confirmation and correction outrank automated interpretation. Preserve the original observation and the correction so the history remains explainable.

## One source of truth

The canonical Brand Pack is framework neutral. Exporters transform the pack; they do not reinterpret the website.

## Ask only consequential questions

Do not make the owner review every scraped value. Prioritize decisions that materially affect identity, trust, accessibility, or implementation.

## Behavior carries the brand

Brand fidelity includes controls, waiting, failure, correction, cancellation, and recovery—not only color, typography, and friendly copy.

## Accessible adaptation is not betrayal

When observed brand usage creates an accessibility failure, preserve the evidence, disclose the conflict, and propose a safer implementation rather than silently copying or silently changing it.

## Safe continuation

Partial imports, blocked pages, and missing assets should preserve valid work and offer a clear next step. Do not convert missing evidence into invented certainty.

## Reversibility

Users must be able to revisit source selection, normalization, interpretations, and owner decisions without restarting the import.

## Honest status

Clearly distinguish provisional, owner reviewed, corrected, blocked, and production-ready states. Do not imply certification, endorsement, or official LumynQ runtime integration.

## Evidence status

These principles are **Observed—plan** and serve as implementation constraints for v0.1.
