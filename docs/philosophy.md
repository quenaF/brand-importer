# Philosophy

Brand Importer is built around one operating idea:

> Ephemeral intelligence. Portable ownership. Clean disconnection.

## The importer is a temporary bridge

Brand Importer connects to authorized sources, observes evidence, proposes interpretations, supports review, exports a portable result, and disconnects.

It is not a CRM, tenant registry, analytics platform, long-term brand memory, or permanent relationship manager.

```text
Connect → Observe → Propose → Review → Export → Dispose
```

## Product promises

1. The importer will not invent an organization's identity.
2. Every consequential conclusion is traceable to evidence or explicitly labeled as inference.
3. Owner decisions are respected throughout the active session and exported bundle.
4. Brand identity remains separate from application-domain logic.
5. Accepted outputs are portable and owned by the developer or organization.
6. The importer retains no organizational memory after disposal.
7. Examples and prior tenants are never used as silent fallback identity or content.
8. Relevance does not equal permission: imagery recommendations never establish reuse rights.

## Fresh means fresh

A future import is a new experience. The importer does not silently remember prior decisions, compare historical imports, or carry old approvals forward.

A later product version may allow the user to explicitly supply a previous runtime brand, Experience Profile, or Domain Adapter for comparison within one new session. That is user-provided context, not importer memory.

## Evidence before confidence

Frequency is not importance. A product catalog may contain hundreds of images while a small program page contains the photographs most relevant to a youth-program preview. The importer preserves the full catalog but uses source context, semantic classification, and an Experience Profile to surface a diverse review plan.

## Human authority

The importer may rank, infer, and recommend. It cannot confirm trademark permission, image rights, consent, brand ownership, or official affiliation. Those decisions remain human responsibilities.

## Domain separation

The brand website answers: “How does this organization express itself?”

The host project answers: “What software experience are we building?”

The Experience Profile and Domain Adapter connect those answers without teaching Brand Importer to become a camp app, store, clinic, marketplace, or any other domain.
